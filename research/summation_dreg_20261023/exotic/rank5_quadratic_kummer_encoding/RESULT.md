## Falsifier outcome (per engine; falsifier text unchanged)
- msolve F4: d_solv(c1) < d_solv(b) and d_solv(c2) < d_solv(b) in all 5 configs -> falsifier NOT met on this engine ("lowers").
- Singular degBound: c1 > b in all 4 finished configs (raises); c2 < b at d=4 (12 vs 13) and d=8 (24 vs 25, both primes), c2 > b at d=6 (22 vs 19) -> mixed, no consistent lowering.
- Bezout: rises strongly for c1 and c2 (x9 to x32), no drop.
Overall: engine-dependent / inconclusive. The lever is not cleanly falsified (msolve disagrees) and not supported (Singular does not agree; Bezout grows).

## Interpretation (separate from measurement)
- The msolve "lowering" is largely structural: F4 degree is bounded below by the largest input degree, which is d (field equations) for b,c1,c2 but 12-16 for the unsplit S4 (a); c2 sits exactly at that floor (offset 0) so the metric cannot show anything more for it. It reflects lower input degree, not an easier ideal; the ideal (vdim) is unchanged and variables grow 4 -> 6 -> 16.
- Singular, which measures when the zero-dimensional basis is reached, shows no robust gain and a clear penalty for c1; the extra algebraically dependent auxiliaries do not help, consistent with the program's prior. Small differences for c2 (1 or 2 degrees at d=4,8) are within the noise of one instance per config; nothing says they generalise.
- Offsets are not constant in d on Singular (b: 9..35), so d is not simply additive; the confound is real and only partly separated by the d in {4,6} variants.
